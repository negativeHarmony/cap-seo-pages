How to Wear a Beanie With Thinning Hair

A beanie works well with thinning hair when it's a medium-weight knit with a soft lining that sits snugly without pressing your hair flat. Cuffed and slouchy styles both work, but how far down you wear it changes the whole look. Choose the fabric carefully, because rough wool can feel scratchy and leave fine hair full of static.

What to look for

  • Choose a beanie lined with fleece or soft cotton to cut down on itch and static
  • Pick merino or a cotton blend over coarse acrylic, which can cling to fine hair
  • Wear a cuffed beanie just above the eyebrows for a neat, deliberate look
  • Try a short fisherman-style beanie that sits above the ears if a full beanie feels like too much
  • Avoid very tight knits that leave a deep line in your hair when you take them off
