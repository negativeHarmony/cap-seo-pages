Should You Wear a Hat on a First Date?

Yes, you can wear a hat on a first date if it fits the setting and your style, as long as it's clean, well-fitted and you're ready to take it off indoors if the venue calls for it. A hat you already wear often will feel natural and help you relax. What matters most is that you look comfortable, not hidden.

What to look for

  • Wear a hat that's part of your usual style rather than something new you're unsure about
  • Match it to the venue, with a cap for a daytime walk and a smarter hat or none for a nice restaurant
  • Take it off at a sit-down dinner indoors, and do it casually without making a moment of it
  • Keep your hair neat underneath in case the hat comes off
  • Avoid a cap pulled low over your eyes, since eye contact matters more than the hat
