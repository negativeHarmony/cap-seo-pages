Fitted, Snapback or Strapback: Which Cap Closure Is Right for You?

A fitted cap gives the cleanest, most secure fit if you know your exact size, while snapbacks and strapbacks let you adjust the fit and are more forgiving. Snapbacks use plastic snaps for quick changes, and strapbacks use a fabric or leather strap for finer adjustment and a smarter look. The right choice depends on how sure you are of your size and how you want the back of the cap to look.

What to look for

  • Choose a fitted cap if you want no gap at the back and know your size for certain
  • Pick a strapback if you want small adjustments and a neater look from behind
  • Go for a snapback if you share caps or like changing how firmly it sits
  • Check that the closure sits flat and doesn't dig into the back of your head
  • If the opening at the back shows more scalp than you'd like, a fitted cap closes that gap completely
