What Hat Should a Man Wear to a Wedding?

For most weddings, a man should choose a dressier hat like a felt or straw fedora, a trilby or a smart flat cap rather than a baseball cap. Match the hat to the dress code and the season. It's also traditional to take it off indoors, especially during the ceremony.

What to look for

  • Check the invitation's dress code before choosing a hat
  • Wear a straw or lightweight felt hat for outdoor summer weddings and a wool felt hat in cooler months
  • Remove your hat indoors and during the ceremony unless your faith or culture calls for keeping it on
  • Save baseball caps for very casual or beach weddings, and only if the couple is relaxed about it
  • Pick a hat colour that ties in with your shoes or belt so it looks part of the outfit
