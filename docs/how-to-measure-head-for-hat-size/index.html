How to Measure Your Head for the Right Hat Size

To measure your head for a hat, wrap a soft tape measure around your head about a finger's width above your eyebrows and ears, keeping it level all the way round. That number is what you match to a size chart. Getting it right means a cap that sits where you want it instead of riding up or slipping down.

What to look for

  • Use a soft sewing tape, or a piece of string you then lay against a ruler
  • Measure over the widest part of your head, just above the ears and across the forehead
  • Take the measurement two or three times and use the largest result
  • If you fall between sizes, go up and use the adjustable strap or a sizing insert to fine-tune
  • Measure with your hair as you usually wear it, since a fresh cut or longer hair can change the reading
