Which Cap Style Suits Your Face Shape?

The right cap for your face shape is the one that balances it: taller crowns suit round faces, lower crowns suit long faces, and softer, curved styles suit square faces. Oval faces can wear most styles. It's a quick way to make any cap look like it was chosen for you.

What to look for

  • Round face: choose a high, structured crown and a straighter brim to add length
  • Long face: go for a low-profile crown and wear the brim slightly lower to shorten the look
  • Square face: pick a rounded crown and a curved brim to soften a strong jaw
  • Oval face: you can wear most shapes, so let your outfit and the occasion decide
  • Check your choice in a full-length mirror, not just up close, to judge proportions
