Stylish Hats for Men Over 50

Men over 50 tend to look best in hats with clean lines and quality fabrics, such as a plain wool or cotton cap, a flat cap, or a felt hat for cooler weather. Skip loud logos and dead-flat brims, which can look like they belong to someone half your age. Muted colours and good materials do most of the work.

What to look for

  • Choose plain caps in navy, charcoal, olive or tan without large logos
  • Try a tweed or wool flat cap for an easy step up from a baseball cap
  • Pick a leather or fabric strap over plastic snaps for a more refined finish
  • Match the hat to the outfit, with a cap for a polo and a felt hat for a coat
  • Invest in one or two good-quality hats rather than lots of cheap ones
