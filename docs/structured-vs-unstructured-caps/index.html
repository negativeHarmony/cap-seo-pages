Structured vs Unstructured Caps Explained

A structured cap has a stiffened front panel that holds its shape and stands tall, while an unstructured cap is soft and sits lower and closer to your head. Structured caps look sharper and keep a defined crown, and unstructured caps feel relaxed and lived-in. Which one suits you comes down to the look you want and how much height you'd like on top.

What to look for

  • Choose a structured cap if you want a crisp front that keeps its height all day
  • Pick an unstructured cap for a laid-back look that hugs the head more closely
  • Wear an unstructured cap if high-front caps feel too tall or tight at the forehead
  • Store structured caps on a shelf or hook so the front panel doesn't get crushed
  • Go unstructured if you want a cap you can fold into a bag without worry
