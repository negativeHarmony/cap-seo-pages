The Best Hats for Bald Men

The best hats for a bald head have a soft inner lining, a snug but not tight fit, and a shape that suits your face, from classic caps to flat caps and beanies. Without hair as a cushion, fit and fabric matter more, so it's worth being a little choosier. The good news is that a clean head lets the hat's shape do all the work.

What to look for

  • Look for flat-stitched or seamless crowns so seams don't leave marks on bare skin
  • Choose a cap with a soft cotton or moisture-wicking sweatband that won't rub bare skin
  • Try a flat cap or newsboy for a sharper look that doesn't read as covering up
  • Pick deeper crowns over shallow ones so the hat sits securely without perching
  • Keep a lightweight beanie for cooler days, as a bare scalp feels the cold faster
