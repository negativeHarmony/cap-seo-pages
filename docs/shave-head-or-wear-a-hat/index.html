Should You Shave Your Head or Wear a Hat?

You don't have to choose just one: many men shave their head for a clean everyday look and still wear hats for sun, cold or style. Shaving suits you if you like low upkeep and a bold look, while a hat suits you if you want flexibility and aren't ready for a big change. Trying a very short clipper cut first is an easy middle step.

What to look for

  • Try a short clipper cut before going fully shaved to see how you feel about the look
  • Plan to wear a hat outdoors in strong sun if you shave, so the two go together
  • If you shave, expect your caps to fit looser and adjust the strap or size down
  • If you keep your hair, choose hats that look intentional rather than worn only to cover up
  • Ask a barber for an honest opinion on your head shape before deciding
