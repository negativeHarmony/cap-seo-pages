The Best Caps for a Thinning Crown

A thinning crown is one of the most common places hair loss shows first, and the right cap covers it completely without looking like you're hiding anything. The key is a structured, mid-to-high crown that sits over the top of the head rather than perching on it.

What to look for

  • Choose a structured front panel so the cap keeps its shape over the crown
  • Look for a mid or high crown height; low-profile caps can leave the crown exposed
  • Pick breathable cotton or a mesh back for all-day comfort
  • Get an adjustable strap so the cap sits snug without pressing on the scalp
