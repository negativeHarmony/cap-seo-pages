How to Choose a Cap That Doesn't Make Your Head Sweat

The caps least likely to leave you sweaty are made from lightweight, moisture-wicking fabrics with mesh panels or vent holes that let heat escape. A good sweatband and a lighter colour also make a noticeable difference on hot days. For runs and workouts, a low-profile, quick-drying cap that stays put is the best bet.

What to look for

  • Look for performance polyester or lightweight cotton blends rather than heavy twill or wool
  • Choose caps with mesh back panels or laser-cut vent holes for airflow
  • Check for a soft, absorbent sweatband that keeps moisture off your forehead
  • Pick a lighter colour for strong sun, since dark caps hold more heat
  • For running, choose an unstructured, quick-drying cap with an adjustable strap so it doesn't bounce
