How to Wash a Baseball Cap Without Ruining It

The safest way to wash a baseball cap is by hand in cool water with a little mild detergent, gently scrubbing the sweatband with a soft brush. Washing machines and dryers can bend the brim and shrink the crown, so skip them. Let the cap air-dry over a rounded shape so it keeps its form.

What to look for

  • Check the care label first, and spot-clean only if it says not to soak the cap
  • For sweat stains on the band, add a little baking soda to the cool soapy water
  • Use a soft toothbrush on the sweatband and seams, working gently in small circles
  • Rinse well, press out water with a towel and never wring the cap
  • Dry it over a balled-up towel or a bowl, out of direct heat, so the crown keeps its shape
