Can You Wear a Cap to Work?

Yes, in many workplaces you can wear a cap, especially in casual, creative, outdoor or trade jobs, but it's best to check your company's dress code first. Office settings with client meetings are usually stricter. If caps are allowed, a plain, clean cap in a neutral colour looks the most professional.

What to look for

  • Check the dress code or ask your manager directly if you're unsure
  • Take your cap off in formal meetings, interviews and client presentations
  • Choose a plain cap with no slogans or large logos for work
  • Keep a work cap clean and in good shape, since a faded or stained one looks careless
  • If caps aren't allowed indoors, wear one for your commute and keep a small comb in your bag
