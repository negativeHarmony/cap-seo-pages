How to Wear a Cap With Glasses

To wear a cap with glasses, set the cap so its band sits just above the arms of your glasses rather than pressing them into the sides of your head. A cap that isn't too tight at the sides stops that pinching feeling behind your ears. The brim should also clear the top of your frames so it doesn't knock them down.

What to look for

  • Put your glasses on first, then lower the cap until the band sits just above the arms
  • Loosen the strap slightly if you feel pressure behind your ears after an hour
  • Choose a curved brim if you wear sunglasses, as it shades the lenses and cuts glare
  • Pick a cap with a slightly higher crown if a low one pushes down on your frames
  • Hook sunglasses on your shirt, not on the brim, so the cap doesn't stretch out of shape
