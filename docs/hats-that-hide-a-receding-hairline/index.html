Hats That Hide a Receding Hairline

A receding hairline shows at the temples and front, so the most important part of the hat is how low and how wide the front sits. A cap or hat with a deeper fit at the forehead covers the temples naturally, so it simply looks like a well-fitted hat.

What to look for

  • Choose a deeper fit that sits lower on the forehead
  • A curved brim shades and frames the temples
  • Avoid shallow 'dad caps' that sit high on the head
  • Size up slightly if the front rides up when you move
