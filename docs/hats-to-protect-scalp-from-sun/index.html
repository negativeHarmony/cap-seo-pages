Choosing a Hat That Keeps the Sun Off Your Scalp

The best hat for keeping sun off your scalp has a tightly woven fabric with no gaps over the crown and a brim wide enough to shade your face and neck. A UPF rating on the label tells you how much sun the fabric blocks. For long days outdoors, a bucket hat or wide-brim hat covers more than a standard cap.

What to look for

  • Hold the hat up to the light, and if you can see light through the weave, sun gets through too
  • Check the label for a UPF rating when you'll be outside for hours
  • Skip caps with mesh panels or vent holes over the crown when the sun is strong
  • Choose a bucket hat or wide brim for beach days, hikes and gardening
  • Pick a cap with a longer brim if you prefer the cap look but want more shade on your face
