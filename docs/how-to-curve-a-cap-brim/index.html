How to Curve a Cap Brim

To curve a cap brim, gently bend it with both hands into an arch, then hold the shape by tucking it into a mug or securing it with a rubber band overnight. Work slowly, as most brims have a cardboard or plastic insert that creases if you force it. A soft, even curve tends to look the most natural.

What to look for

  • Bend the brim little by little over a few minutes instead of in one hard fold
  • Place the brim inside a mug or around a drinking glass overnight to set the curve
  • Use a wide rubber band around the brim to hold the shape while it sets
  • Check the brim from the front in a mirror so both sides curve evenly
  • Go for a gentle curve that frames your face rather than a narrow tunnel shape
